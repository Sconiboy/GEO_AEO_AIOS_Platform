# Source-First Client Experience

## The One-Sentence Product Promise

> **We show the sources an AI answer visibly surfaced, verify what those sources actually say, compare that public proof with yours, and turn the gaps into an ethical action plan.**

The client receives a clear evidence view built around four questions:

1. **Who showed up in the sampled answer?**
2. **Which exact sources did the answer visibly show?**
3. **What does each source establish, and where does the evidence stop?**
4. **What legitimate evidence should the client improve, create, or earn next?**

## Client-Facing Flow

| Client sees | Platform shows | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The answer** | The exact approved buyer question, model surface, timestamp, visible answer excerpt, and explicit URLs. | This capture records one answer at one point in time. |
| **Source map** | Every explicit answer URL, named entity, publisher relationship, source type, visible citation status, and verification status. | The map covers the sources visibly surfaced in this answer. |
| **Evidence comparison** | Side-by-side verified passages from competitor, client, and independent sources where available. | First-party statements remain publisher evidence until independent corroboration exists. |
| **Action plan** | One evidence gap, one legitimate next action, a success artifact, an owner, and a review date. | The plan tracks evidence progress across the defined sample. |

## The Source Evidence Card

Every surfaced URL receives a plain-language card. This is the core of the product.

| Field | Required client-facing language |
| --- | --- |
| **Appeared in the answer?** | `Yes — explicit URL shown` / `No — independently collected context` |
| **Who owns it?** | `Competitor-owned`, `Client-owned`, `Independent editorial`, `Community discussion`, `Review platform`, or `Unknown` |
| **What type of source is it?** | First-party category page, case study, documentation, review, forum discussion, editorial comparison, partner page, etc. |
| **What does it say?** | Exact verified passage, not a paraphrase presented as quotation. |
| **Why it matters here** | Narrow connection to the approved buyer question. |
| **Evidence boundary** | The specific conclusion supported by the passage and the decision that still needs more evidence. |
| **Evidence state** | Explicitly cited and opened-verified; cited but unverified; independently collected and opened-verified; or proposed only. |

## Evidence-Gap-to-Action Translation

The report should translate gaps into **proof work** and **legitimate exposure work**, never manipulation.

| Verified gap type | Ethical action | Completion artifact | Invalid shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competitor has a clear category-specific page; client evidence is generic. | Build or improve one genuinely useful category page with accurate scope, method, examples, and ownership. | Opened-verifiable page with factual review. | Keyword stuffing or thin AI-generated pages. |
| Competitor has real customer proof; client has unverified claims. | Gather permissioned case studies, attributed results, or useful documentation. | Approved, attributable proof asset. | Invented testimonials or inflated outcomes. |
| Competitor has independent discussion or editorial coverage; client has none in sample. | Earn relevant coverage, partner references, expert contributions, or helpful community participation. | Real third-party URL with disclosed relationship. | Paid undisclosed seeding, fake Reddit posts, or review manipulation. |
| The answer sample is too thin to distinguish patterns. | Expand the approved query and model-surface panel before changing strategy. | Reproducible capture set with exact artifacts. | Treating one answer as a trend. |

## 90-Day Client Conversation

The executive conversation should be simple:

> “We show the public evidence that appeared around the answer, identify the proof your market can verify, and build a repeatable plan to strengthen that proof. The defined sample gives your team a clear measurement baseline.”

| Period | Client decision | Work | Evidence of completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 0–30 | Approve evidence priorities. | Verify existing proof, remove ambiguous claims, and close the clearest category-page gap. | Opened and reviewed client evidence set. |
| Days 31–60 | Approve exposure targets. | Publish useful proof, obtain permissioned customer evidence, and pursue legitimate relevant references. | Verified public source and internal approval trail. |
| Days 61–90 | Approve measurement review. | Re-run the declared sample and review changed citations, sources, and unresolved gaps. | Comparable artifact bundle and human review record. |

## Operator Guardrail

The operator leads with **what was observed** before explaining **what may be worth doing**. Sources surfaced by the answer enter the candidate-review queue. Separately collected pages remain context until a human reviewer connects them to a documented evidence question.
